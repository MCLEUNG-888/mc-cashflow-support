MC CashFlow Support

Help with app use, transaction records, access, privacy, exports, and technical issues.

Contact

Email myskyocean2024@gmail.com. Describe the issue and the result you expected.

Useful details for a bug report

  • MC CashFlow app version
  • iOS version and device model
  • Steps that reproduce the issue
  • The exact message shown
  • A screenshot, only if it contains no sensitive information

Protect sensitive information

Do not email passwords, full payment-card numbers, bank-login credentials, authentication secrets, or private encryption keys. Remove financial details and personal information from screenshots unless they are essential to the request.

Privacy enquiries

The current app has no automated privacy-request or cloud-account portal. For questions about app privacy or information you previously sent directly to Support, use the support email above.
